# Jan 'Bearlin' Malicki – Portfolio

Personal portfolio website of Jan Malicki, Game Designer & Game Producer: https://herrbearlin.github.io

## Pages

- **[Home](https://herrbearlin.github.io/index.html)** – An introduction to who I am, my most recent games, my hobbies and how to contact me.
- **[Portfolio](https://herrbearlin.github.io/pages/portfolio.html)** – My recent commercial and non-commercial projects with details of what I did on each, plus examples of my design, production and writing work and the tools I use.
- **[All Games](https://herrbearlin.github.io/pages/all-games.html)** – The complete list of games I have worked on, with visit counts and links to play them.
- **[Contact](https://herrbearlin.github.io/pages/contact.html)** – Send me an email straight from the site or connect with me on LinkedIn and GitHub.
